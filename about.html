About Us

Quality You Can Trust, From Farm to Table

For over 25 years, Qumeshtorja Vita has been Kosovo's most trusted dairy brand. We partner with 200+ family farms, process milk within hours of collection, and uphold the strictest quality standards. Our mission is simple: bring the freshest, purest dairy products from our farms to your family table every single day.

FM

Local Family Farms

We work directly with over 200 family farms across Kosovo, ensuring fair trade prices and the freshest milk collected every morning. These are multi-generational farming families who share our commitment to quality and animal welfare.

ISO

Certified Quality

ISO 22000 certified facility with state-of-the-art pasteurization and packaging lines. Every batch undergoes rigorous testing for purity, fat content, and microbiological safety before it ever reaches a store shelf.

NAT

No Compromises

No artificial hormones, no preservatives, no additives. Just pure, natural dairy products made the way nature intended. Our cows graze on open pastures and receive regular veterinary care to ensure their health and wellbeing.

Our Heritage

A Legacy of Freshness Since 1995

What began as a small family dairy in the heart of Kosovo has grown into the nation's most beloved dairy brand.

The story of Qumeshtorja Vita begins in 1995, when the Devolli family set out with a simple but powerful vision: to bring fresh, natural dairy to every Kosovar household. Starting with a single milk collection route and a handful of dedicated farmers, Vita quickly earned a reputation for unmatched freshness and purity.

Over the decades, we have grown from that small family operation into Kosovo's largest and most trusted dairy producer — but our values have never changed. We still collect milk each morning from local family farms. We still process it within hours rather than days. And we still test every single batch to ensure it meets our rigorous standards.

Today, Qumeshtorja Vita is part of the Devolli Group, one of Kosovo's most respected business conglomerates. This backing gives us the resources to invest in cutting-edge technology, sustainable practices, and community development — while preserving the family values that made us who we are.

Our Commitment

Sustainability & Community

We believe that good business means taking care of the land, the animals, and the people who make it all possible.

ECO

Eco-Friendly Packaging

All our bottles, cartons, and containers are designed for recyclability. We are actively reducing plastic across our supply chain and investing in biodegradable alternatives for the future.

COW

Animal Welfare First

Our partner farms practice free-range grazing and provide regular veterinary care. Healthy, happy cows produce the best milk — it is that simple.

SCH

School Milk Program

We supply free fresh milk to primary schools across Kosovo, along with nutrition education and farm field trips that teach children where their food comes from.

JOB

Local Employment

Over 500 Kosovars work across our farms, factories, and distribution network. We invest in training, career development, and fair wages for every employee.
