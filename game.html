Educational Game

Vita: Farm to Fridge

Discover how fresh Vita milk is made — from the farm to your fridge! Play through 5 interactive stages: feed the cows on Kosovo pastures, time the milking perfectly, cool the fresh milk to 4C, navigate pasteurization at 72C, and bottle the final product for delivery. Learn real dairy science while having fun!

Click / Tap: Interact Space: Jump / Dodge Drag: Feed Cows
How to Play

Game Guide

Stage 1: Feed the Cows

Drag hay bales to grazing cows. Each cow needs 3 feedings to be ready for milking. Feed all 6 cows to advance!

Stage 2: Milking Time

Click the cow's udder in the pasture to extract milk. Watch the milk stream into the bucket. Fill it to 100% to advance!

Stage 3: Cool the Milk

Move your bucket to catch falling ice blocks and cool the milk to 4C. Dodge red heat blocks — they raise the temperature!

Stage 4: Pasteurization

Click inside the milk tank to create heat bursts that zap bacteria! Keep the temperature right at 72C for the best results.

Stage 5: Pack & Deliver

Grab milk bottles from the counter and drop them into the Vita shipping crate. Pack all 12 bottles to complete the journey!
