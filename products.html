Product Details

Why Our Products Stand Out

Every Vita product is the result of decades of dairy expertise, the freshest local milk, and an uncompromising commitment to quality.

Fresh Milk 3.2%

Our signature whole milk is sourced from grass-fed cows and pasteurized to perfection. Rich in calcium and vitamin D with a creamy taste that Kosovars have loved for generations. Available in 1L and 2L bottles. No additives, no preservatives — just pure fresh milk.

Calcium RichVitamin DNo Additives

Yogurt 1.5%

Low-fat creamy yogurt made with live active cultures for gut health. Packed with protein and probiotics, our yogurt is a nutritious breakfast staple. Smooth texture with no added sugar — perfect on its own or with fresh fruit and honey.

ProbioticsHigh ProteinLow Fat

Ayran 1.2%

The classic Balkan refreshment — Vita yogurt blended with water and a pinch of salt. Light, tangy, and incredibly refreshing. Ayran is the perfect companion to any meal, especially grilled meats and traditional pies. A taste of Kosovar culture in every sip.

RefreshingElectrolytesTraditional

Kos 3.2% 400g

Thick, strained traditional yogurt with a velvety texture and higher protein content than regular yogurt. Essential for traditional Kosovar dishes, mezze platters, and burek. Its rich, tangy flavor makes it a versatile ingredient in both savory and sweet recipes.

StrainedExtra ProteinVersatile

Cooking Cream 1L

Heat-stable cream formulated for cooking — it will not split or curdle in hot dishes. Ideal for creamy soups, rich pasta sauces, casseroles, and gratins. Professional chefs across Kosovo trust Vita Cooking Cream for consistent results every time.

Heat StableProfessionalRich

Whipping Cream

Premium whipping cream that whips to stiff peaks every time. The secret behind Kosovo's finest cakes, pastries, and dessert toppings. Its high fat content and smooth consistency make it ideal for both home bakers and professional pastry chefs.

BakingDessertsPremium

Vita Cheese

Artisan white cheese crafted from the freshest Vita milk and aged for deep, rich flavor. A semi-hard, slightly salty cheese that is perfect for breakfast with bread and tomatoes, crumbled over salads, or layered into traditional byrek. A true taste of Kosovo.

ArtisanAgedKosovar Classic
