Cooking Tips

Tips from Our Kitchen

Small details make the difference between a good dish and a great one. Here are our tried-and-tested tips for cooking and baking with Vita products.

Perfect Pancakes

Use room-temperature Vita milk for fluffier batter. Let the batter rest 10 minutes before cooking — this allows the flour to hydrate fully. Flip when bubbles form on the surface. Serve with fresh seasonal fruit and a drizzle of honey for a breakfast that the whole family will love.

Prep: 10 minCook: 15 minEasy

Baking Cheesecake

Always use Vita Cooking Cream at room temperature for the creamiest texture. Bake in a water bath to prevent cracking. Cool gradually in the oven with the door cracked open. Chill overnight in the refrigerator — patience is the secret ingredient.

Prep: 20 minBake: 55 minMedium

Alfredo Sauce

Warm Vita Whipping Cream gently — never let it boil or it may separate. Toss the pasta directly in the sauce with a splash of starchy pasta water for the perfect consistency. Finish with freshly grated parmesan and cracked black pepper.

Prep: 5 minCook: 15 minEasy

Traditional Ayran

Use ice-cold Vita yogurt and sparkling water for extra refreshment. The classic ratio is 2 parts yogurt to 1 part water, but adjust to your preference. Blend with fresh mint for a modern twist. Best served immediately in a chilled glass on a hot summer day.

Prep: 2 minNo CookEasy

Burek me Kos

Layer thin filo pastry sheets with generous amounts of Vita Kos yogurt for an authentic, tangy flavor. Brush each layer with melted butter for that golden, crispy crust. Bake at 200C until deep golden brown. Let rest 5 minutes before slicing for clean cuts.

Prep: 25 minBake: 35 minMedium

Homemade Ice Cream

Whip Vita Whipping Cream to soft peaks before folding in sweetened condensed milk and your favorite flavors. Freeze in a shallow container, stirring every 30 minutes for the first 2 hours to break up ice crystals. The result is incredibly creamy ice cream with no special equipment needed.

Prep: 15 minFreeze: 4+ hrsEasy
