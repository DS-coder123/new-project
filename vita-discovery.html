Station 1 — The Farm
Where does Vita milk come from? Learn about dairy farming.

🥛 Milk Collection

Vita sources fresh milk daily from local Kosovo farms. Cows are milked twice a day — each cow produces about 25 liters. The milk is cooled to 4°C within minutes to preserve freshness.

🐮 Cow Breeds

Vita works with farms raising Holstein and Simmental cows — breeds known for high-quality milk rich in protein (3.2%) and butterfat, perfect for making cheese and yogurt.

🌿 Sustainable Farming

Vita partner farms use sustainable practices: rotational grazing, natural feed, and animal welfare standards. Happy cows produce better milk!

🚛 Farm to Factory

Refrigerated Vita trucks collect milk every morning. The journey from farm to the Vita dairy takes under 2 hours — ensuring peak freshness for every Vita product.

Station 2 — Dairy Science
What happens to milk inside the Vita lab? Test your knowledge.

🌡️ Pasteurization

Milk is heated to 72°C for 15 seconds, then rapidly cooled. This kills harmful bacteria while preserving taste and nutrients — a process discovered by Louis Pasteur in 1864.

🦠 Fermentation Magic

Yogurt and kos are made by adding live bacterial cultures (Lactobacillus bulgaricus and Streptococcus thermophilus) to warm milk. They convert lactose into lactic acid — creating that tangy flavor!
